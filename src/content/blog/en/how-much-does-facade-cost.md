## How Much Does a House Facade Cost in 2026? Complete Pricing Guide

Planning insulation and facade finishing is one of the most important investment decisions for a homeowner. The cost of a facade depends on many factors, and price differences between quotes can reach as much as 40-60%. In this guide, we present reliable, up-to-date pricing data for 2026, so you can plan your budget wisely.

## What Makes Up the Cost of a Facade?

The total cost of facade work is the sum of several main components. Understanding each of them allows you to better evaluate the quotes you receive and avoid unpleasant surprises.

### 1. Insulation Materials

Thermal insulation material typically accounts for 25-35% of the total investment cost. Current prices per square meter of material (excluding labor):

- **White polystyrene EPS 70 (15 cm)** — 35-50 PLN/m²
- **Graphite polystyrene EPS 032 (15 cm)** — 55-75 PLN/m²
- **Facade mineral wool (15 cm)** — 70-110 PLN/m²
- **Lamellar rock wool (15 cm)** — 90-130 PLN/m²

### 2. Renders and Facade Finishes

Cost of facade render including primer and reinforcing mesh:

- **Acrylic render** — 25-40 PLN/m²
- **Silicone render** — 35-55 PLN/m²
- **Silicate render** — 30-50 PLN/m²
- **Mineral render** — 20-35 PLN/m²

### 3. Labor

Labor costs in Małopolska in 2026 are as follows:

- **ETICS system insulation (complete work)** — 80-140 PLN/m²
- **Insulation installation only** — 35-55 PLN/m²
- **Render application with mesh** — 40-60 PLN/m²
- **Detail work (sills, corners, reveals)** — 60-120 PLN/lm

### 4. Scaffolding

Scaffolding rental and installation is a cost that many investors forget about:

- **Frame scaffolding rental** — 12-20 PLN/m² per month
- **Assembly and disassembly** — 15-25 PLN/m²
- **Safety nets** — 3-6 PLN/m²

Typical rental period for a single-family house is 4-8 weeks, giving a total scaffolding cost of around 3,000-7,000 PLN.

## Factors Affecting the Price

### Insulation Thickness

Current technical requirements (WT 2021) set the thermal transmittance coefficient for walls at U ≤ 0.20 W/(m²·K). In practice, this means:

- For a ceramic block wall — minimum 15 cm of graphite polystyrene or 18 cm of wool
- For a 24 cm aerated concrete wall — minimum 12 cm of graphite polystyrene
- When applying for a subsidy from the **Clean Air** program — often a thickness ensuring U ≤ 0.15 W/(m²·K) is required

Every additional 2 cm of insulation increases the material cost by approximately 8-15 PLN/m².

### Building Complexity

Houses with many windows, bay windows, balconies, and architectural details are more expensive to insulate by as much as 20-30%. Window reveal work, corners, and roof junctions require precision and experience.

### Location and Accessibility

In large metropolitan areas (Kraków, Warszawa, Wrocław) labor costs are 10-20% higher than in smaller towns. Difficult access to walls (e.g., close neighboring buildings) can also increase scaffolding costs.

### Existing Facade Condition

If the building requires removal of old render, crack repair, or surface leveling, the cost increases by 30-60 PLN/m².

## When Are Facade Prices Lowest?

The facade season runs from April to October. The best prices can be obtained:

- **In autumn (October-November)** — crews look for jobs at the end of the season
- **In winter (December-February)** — companies offer discounts of up to 10-15% on materials with spring delivery
- **When signing a contract in advance** — booking a crew 3-4 months before work begins gives room for negotiation

## What Should a Professional Quote Include?

A reliable facade quote must include:

- **Accurate measurements** — measured on site, not from plans
- **Material specification** — specific products with manufacturer name and parameters
- **Itemized breakdown** — separate materials, labor, scaffolding, and detail work
- **Work schedule** — accounting for technological drying breaks
- **Warranty terms** — minimum 5 years on workmanship, materials per manufacturer
- **Payment method** — post-completion measurement vs. lump sum

### Red Flags in a Quote

Be cautious of quotes that:

- Do not include an itemized breakdown (one price "for everything")
- Do not specify materials or list no-name products
- Are significantly cheaper than competitors (difference above 25%)
- Require 50% or more deposit before work begins
- Do not offer a written warranty

## How to Save on a Facade Without Sacrificing Quality?

1. **Take advantage of subsidies** — the Clean Air program offers funding up to 37,500 PLN for thermal modernization, and up to 69,000 PLN in the enhanced tier
2. **Plan work comprehensively** — insulating walls, roof, and replacing windows in one project reduces scaffolding and site organization costs
3. **Choose optimal insulation thickness** — increasing thickness beyond the cost-effective optimum (usually 18-20 cm) yields diminishing heating savings
4. **Negotiate material prices** — contractors buy materials at wholesale discounts of 15-30%
5. **Avoid rushing** — ordering during peak season (May-July) means the highest prices

## Summary — Realistic Facade Costs in 2026

For a typical single-family house with a facade area of 180-220 m², you should prepare a budget of:

- **Economy option** (white polystyrene, acrylic render) — **35,000-55,000 PLN**
- **Optimal option** (graphite polystyrene, silicone render) — **50,000-75,000 PLN**
- **Premium option** (mineral wool, silicone/silicate render) — **65,000-95,000 PLN**

Remember that a well-executed facade is an investment that pays for itself through 30-60% lower heating bills and increases the property value by 10-15%.
