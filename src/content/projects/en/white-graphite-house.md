## About the Project

Comprehensive facade work on a single-family house in a modern white-graphite color scheme with brown soffit and roof flashing elements. The house features balconies with metal railings.

### Scope of Work

- ETICS insulation system — graphite polystyrene
- White and graphite silicone render
- Brown sheet metal flashings
- External window sill installation
- Detail and corner finishing
