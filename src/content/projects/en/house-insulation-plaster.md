## About the Project

Comprehensive insulation and facade rendering of a single-family house with a habitable attic. The building received a modern white-graphite color scheme with dark accents around the windows, perfectly matched to the dark roof tiles and brown window joinery.

### Scope of Work

- External wall insulation with polystyrene in the ETICS system
- Silicone render in white (main facade)
- Graphite decorative accents around windows and on gables
- Building plinth finishing
- Sheet metal flashings and external window sills
- Protecting the building surroundings during work

### Final Result

A modern, two-tone facade with distinct graphite accents that visually highlight the building's form. Silicone render ensures durability and self-cleaning properties of the facade for years to come.
