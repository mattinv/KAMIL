## About the Project

Insulation and facade finishing of a single-story house. The client expected a warm, natural color palette with wood-effect elements.

### Scope of Work

- ETICS insulation system — 200mm graphite polystyrene
- White silicone render
- Wood-effect details (Cedral boards)
- Corner flashings in graphite color
