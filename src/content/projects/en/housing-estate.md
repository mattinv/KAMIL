## About the Project

The project encompassed comprehensive facade work for a single-family housing estate. A modern white-graphite color scheme was applied with dark sheet metal flashing elements.

### Scope of Work

- ETICS insulation system — 200mm graphite polystyrene
- Silicone render in two colors
- Glass balustrade installation
- Sheet metal flashings and window sills
- Roof soffit
