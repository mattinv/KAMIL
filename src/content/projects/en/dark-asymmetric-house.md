## About the Project

A modern single-family house with a distinctive asymmetric roof and dark facade. Dark graphite silicone render is the dominant color, contrasting with white elements around the windows. Large glazing on the ground and upper floors emphasizes the modern character of the building.

### Scope of Work

- ETICS insulation system — graphite polystyrene
- Silicone render in dark graphite (RAL 7016)
- White details and window surrounds
- Large glazing with dark aluminum frames
- Sheet metal flashings and parapet work
