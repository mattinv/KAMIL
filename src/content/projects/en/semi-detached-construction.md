## About the Project

Facade work on a semi-detached house during construction. Graphite polystyrene insulation was applied and finished with silicone render in a white-graphite color scheme.

### Scope of Work

- ETICS insulation system — 200mm graphite polystyrene
- Silicone render in two shades
- Glass balustrade installation
- Sheet metal flashings and window sills
