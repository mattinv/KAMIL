## About the Project

Insulation installation on a single-story house using Enerpor polystyrene. The photo shows the stage of mounting insulation boards on the facade with XPS plinth insulation (green color).

### Scope of Work

- Substrate preparation and priming
- Bonding Enerpor polystyrene to external walls
- Plinth insulation with XPS polystyrene
- Installation of starter profiles and mechanical anchoring
- Mesh embedding and rendering (next stage)
